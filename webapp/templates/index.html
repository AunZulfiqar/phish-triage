    What it checks

    Authentication

    SPF, DKIM and DMARC results, plus relaxed identifier alignment — whether the domain that actually authenticated is the one the recipient sees.

    Headers

    Disagreement between From, Reply-To, Return-Path, Message-ID and the display name. Attackers have to make one of them lie.

    URLs

    Anchor text that claims one domain while the href points at another, punycode and mixed-script hosts, brand lookalikes, @ obfuscation, open redirects.

    Attachments

    Executables, double extensions, right-to-left override, macro formats, disk-image containers. Hashed, never opened.

    Content

    Credential forms in the body, text hidden with CSS, zero-width characters, and the payload-free shape of a business-email-compromise opener.

    Not a classifier

    The score is a transparent weighted sum over a published catalogue. Every point traces to a named indicator you can argue with.
